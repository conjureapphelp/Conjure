Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026
Conjure was built with the belief that your private practice deserves complete privacy.
This policy explains, in plain language, what we do and do not collect.
✦ ✦ ✦

1.Who We Are

Conjure is a personal manifestation app designed to support your private practice. It is built and maintained by an independent developer based in India. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data Conjure does and does not collect.

2.What Data We Collect

All information you enter into Conjure — names, journal entries, intentions, scripts, notes, check-ins, signs, tracker data, and any photos — is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Because everything lives on your device, only you can see it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that information is gone.

3.AI Features & How They Work

When you use an AI-powered feature inside Conjure — such as scripting, affirmations, audio meditations, or letter rituals — the text of your request travels through Conjure's secure server (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) on its way to Anthropic, the company that operates the AI model (Claude) which generates the response.

We do not log, store, or read these requests or responses. They pass through our server and are not saved.

Anthropic processes the request to generate a response and, by default, does not use API content to train its models. Anthropic's own privacy practices apply to this brief transmission and can be reviewed at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

4.Device ID & Daily Limits

To enforce daily usage limits fairly (a small number of free AI rituals and audio meditations per day), Conjure generates a random, anonymous device identifier the first time you open the app. This is a random string of characters created on your device — it is not your name, email, phone number, advertising ID, or any other personal identifier, and it cannot be linked back to you.

Our server stores only this random device ID alongside a count of how many AI rituals you have used that day. This counter automatically resets and is deleted within roughly 26 hours.

5.Photos

If you choose to add a photo within Conjure, it is stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or accessible to us or to any third party.

6.Analytics & Tracking

Conjure does not use any analytics tools, tracking pixels, advertising IDs, or third-party monitoring services. We do not know how you use the app, how often you open it, or which features you use. There is no telemetry of any kind.

7.Subscriptions & Payments

If and when paid features become available, all payments will be processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see, store, or handle your payment details at any point.

8.Children

Conjure is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the app, please contact us and we will assist where possible — although, because everything is stored locally on the user's own device, there is no account or server data for us to remove.

9.Your Rights

Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control at all times. You can:

  • Export your data from within the app
  • Clear all data using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything entirely

There is no account to delete because Conjure does not create accounts.

10.Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Significant changes will also be reflected inside the app. Continued use of Conjure after an update means you accept the revised policy.
