Conjure — Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026
1. Who We Are
Conjure is a personal manifestation app designed to support your private practice. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we do and do not collect.
2. What Data We Collect
All information you enter — names, journal entries, intentions, notes, and photos — is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it.
When you use AI-powered features, the text you have entered passes through Conjure's secure server on its way to Anthropic's API, which generates the response. We do not log, store, or read these requests or responses. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to this communication.
3. Photos
If you choose to upload a photo, it is stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or accessible to us or any third party.
4. Analytics
We do not use any analytics tools, tracking pixels, or third-party monitoring. We do not know how you use the app, how often you open it, or what features you use.
5. Subscriptions and Payments
All payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not see, store, or handle your payment details at any point.
6. Children
Conjure is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
7. Your Rights
Since all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control at all times. You can export it, clear it, or uninstall the app to remove it entirely. There is no account to delete because we do not create accounts.
8. Contact
Questions about this policy: conjureapp.help@gmail.com
Conjure is built with the belief that your private practice deserves complete privacy. Nothing you write here is ever judged, stored, or seen by anyone but you.
